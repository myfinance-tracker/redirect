Privacy Policy — Finance Tracker

A personal finance tracking app, shared privately with family. Not a commercial product or public service.

What this app is

Finance Tracker is a personal Android app for tracking your own finances. It is not published in any app store; it is shared privately with a few family members, and each person uses it only on their own phone, for their own money.

What data it accesses

With the account holder's explicit consent (via MitID / bank login through Enable Banking's PSD2 open banking service), the app can read account balance and transaction history for bank accounts the account holder has personally linked. No other accounts or data are accessed.

Where the data goes

  • All financial data is stored locally on the user's own device, in the app's local database.
  • Nothing is uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by the app's developer. Users can't see each other's data.
  • The app does not share, sell, or transmit financial data to any third party, except as described below.
  • The app does not run analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Optional AI features send transaction descriptions and summaries directly from the device (over an encrypted connection) to the AI service chosen in the app — Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic Claude — to suggest categories and generate spending insights.
  • Optional backups are saved where the user chooses: on the device, or in the user's own OneDrive or Google Drive. With a password, the backup file is encrypted.

Data retention and deletion

Data persists locally until the user deletes it (by clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or revoking bank access through Enable Banking / MitID at any time).

Contact

This app is shared privately and has no public support channel.
